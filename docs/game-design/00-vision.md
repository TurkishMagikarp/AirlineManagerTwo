# Vision, world identity and player goals

## Approved
- **GDD-001 — Accessibility principle:** Deep management simulation usable both casually and at expert depth. Ordered, elegant, self-explanatory UI; progressive disclosure rather than mandatory micromanagement.
- **GDD-002 — World:** Real Earth, cities, airports and aircraft, with deliberate gameplay freedom. Realism should have understandable consequences rather than arbitrary restrictions.
- **GDD-004 — New game:** Custom airline founding **and** additional curated scenarios. Beginners receive sensible defaults.
- **GDD-005 — Business model:** No permanently fixed low-cost/full-service class. Strategy emerges dynamically from service, pricing, network, fleet and other choices; mixed businesses allowed.
- **GDD-019 — Long-term play:** Unlimited open sandbox with optional goals, milestones and achievements, no mandatory unlock gates.
- **GDD-028 — Difficulty:** Separately configurable simulation realism, competitive pressure, crisis intensity, and starting conditions; automation level is independent of difficulty.
- **GDD-029 — Era:** Modern baseline that evolves dynamically over decades, no required historical-start era.
- **GDD-040 — Initial datasets:** Real airports, aircraft, airlines and realistic networks where feasible; plausible simulated economic and operational data where verified data are unavailable.
- **GDD-041 — Regulation:** Realistic core of traffic rights, agreements and market access; optional simplification of market access. Aircraft/crew safety constraints are not negotiable.
- **GDD-044 — Geography:** All continents available from the start; adaptive economic detail for performance.
- **GDD-045 — Business activities:** Scheduled passenger, cargo, charter, holiday, regional and ACMI/wet-lease activities, potentially combined.
- **GDD-064 — Founding assistant:** Optional plan produced by management; player can accept, customize or ignore it.

## Design constraints
Free choice must still produce economic and operational consequences. Initial dataset quality/licensing limits must be tracked. A generated world is independent of subsequent changes in real-world data.

## Not yet specified
Precise start calendar year, scope/licensing of initial airline data, country rules, starting capital presets, scenario content, numerical difficulty modifiers, and the detailed founding assistant algorithm.
