# Personnel, executives and labor market

## Approved
- **GDD-012 — Personnel:** Individual employees, career/experience, licences, rosters, working and rest time, unions, strikes, outsourcing and automated workforce planning.
- **GDD-021 — Managers:** Player can delegate entire departments or enterprise; managers respect limits and report reasons.
- **GDD-048 — Analysts:** Individual analyst specializations, experience and training with noisy/non-guaranteed estimates.
- **GDD-051 — Executives:** Individual leadership competence, experience, pay expectations, style, career progression and possible poaching by competitors. **Not** the unchosen complete boardroom politics simulation.
- **GDD-052 — Labor market:** Globally linked hiring, movement, poaching, training supply, regional scarcity and dynamic wages.
- **GDD-053 — Training:** Internal and external training and simulators, cabin and technician qualifications.

## Invariants
Staff have actual training, qualification and time constraints; simulating management autonomy does not excuse violating them. Competing airlines draw from shared labor availability. Better analyst/manager skill changes decision quality statistically, never guarantees a perfect outcome.

## Open
Employee persistence strategy at scale, roster constraint solver, wages and turnover models, training capacity/qualification mapping, cross-border recruitment constraints.
