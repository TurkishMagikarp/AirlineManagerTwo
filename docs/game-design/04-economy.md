# Finance, companies and contracts

## Approved
- **GDD-011 — Enterprise finance:** Balance sheet, income statement, cash flow, loans, bonds, investors, valuation, subsidiaries and acquisitions, explained through an accessible finance hub.
- **GDD-021 — Autonomous management:** May delegate departments and whole company, with player-chosen constraints; no external LLM required.
- **GDD-043 — Insolvency:** Restructuring and rescue options before possible final corporate insolvency and end of company management. No automatic same-world restart was selected.
- **GDD-046 — Contracts:** Shared cross-system contracts, independently acting partners, negotiations, creditworthiness, terms, commitments, default and risk.
- **GDD-050 — Planning:** Multi-year strategies, department budgets, investment programs, target conflicts and management proposals.
- **GDD-056 — Taxes:** Simplified country-dependent corporate income tax rates. No complex transfer pricing or international tax treaty mechanics.
- **GDD-057 — Currency:** **Single uniform in-game currency** for every international transaction. No FX hedging or multiple-currency economy.
- **GDD-058 — M&A:** Full due diligence, financing, regulatory review, integration, fleet/personnel coordination and economic synergies.
- **GDD-059 — Ownership:** Strategic investment, investors' financial/control rights, possible loss of control, partial/full sales of subsidiaries. No mandatory full stock exchange simulation.

## Invariants
Accrual profit differs from cash. All finance entries reconcile. Prices/wages differ regionally **even though currency is uniform**. Acquisition does not erase debt, leases, liabilities or contracts. Synergies require implementation rather than free instant bonuses. Equity issuance and sale of existing shares are distinct.

## Open
Ledger model/period close, tax calendar, debt covenants, bond pricing, contract standard fields, acquisition process and shareholder voting/rights.
