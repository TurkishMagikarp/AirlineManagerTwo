# Studio Lead decisions

Product decisions the Studio Lead made under the owner's delegation. They add to the approved decisions in `docs/game-design/decisions/DECISION-REGISTER.md` and never override them. The owner can revoke any entry.

| ID | Date | Decision | Reason | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LEAD-001 | 2026-11-20 | The playable alpha requires a complete offline custom-airline campaign from founding through the start of Year 3, with a recoverable save, understandable operating and financial outcomes, and working player actions at every step. | Two elapsed years give fleet upkeep, financial commitments and world changes time to matter after the first route opens. | AIR-443 |
| LEAD-002 | 2026-11-20 | When insolvency ends company management, the game shows a persistent end-of-company notice with Load and New Game while keeping the frozen company available for inspection and saving. New Game starts a separate world only after valid founding is submitted. | A player can leave the ended company without losing named saves or accidentally discarding the current state. | AIR-445 |
| LEAD-003 | 2026-11-20 | On first opening Routes for a founded or loaded company, the route planner defaults to that company's home airport and asks the player to choose a destination. Creating a route selects it and brings its editor into view so the player can move straight to fare and schedule setup while retaining manual control. | A player founding away from EDDF is no longer steered toward an out-of-range route or one whose aircraft is parked at the wrong airport, and can reach fare and schedule setup directly after creation. | AIR-447 |
| LEAD-004 | 2026-11-20 | When a player aircraft reaches its wear limit and is grounded for an unscheduled check, Attention identifies the aircraft and opens its Fleet maintenance details, and a time jump stops at the grounding. | The player sees why service stopped and what happens next, even when the check would fit entirely inside the jump. | AIR-449 |
| LEAD-005 | 2026-11-20 | Completed-flight and route profitability rows show total operating revenue alongside flight costs and the result, with ticket, cargo and ancillary receipts available as a breakdown. | The player can reconcile the figures and see how fares, freight and fees contributed to the result. | AIR-451 |
| LEAD-007 | 2026-11-20 | The Save and Load dialogs suspend simulation time while open, and closing them returns control to the active world's own running state and speed. | Players can name or choose a save without flights or financial events changing the company while the time controls are out of reach. | AIR-455 |
| LEAD-008 | 2026-11-20 | From Routes, the player can wind down an active route by releasing its assigned aircraft at their next safe opportunity, then explicitly delete the route while keeping completed results. | A player can withdraw service without trying to catch every aircraft parked at the same moment. | AIR-457 |
| LEAD-009 | 2026-11-21 | When a grounded aircraft is waiting for technicians, Fleet shows the staffing blocker and a direct action to open technician hiring. Fleet shows a release time once the check has technicians assigned. | Players can tell whether to address staffing or advance time. | AIR-459 |
| LEAD-010 | 2026-11-21 | Overview and Finances present the same cumulative company revenue, costs and result, with charter fees counted in revenue and charter penalties counted in costs and identified as such. | A player can reconcile the company result and see which receipts and expenses drove it. | AIR-461 |
| LEAD-011 | 2026-11-21 | When an airline is open, loading a manual save, autosave or backup requires confirmation that names the target and explains that unsaved progress in the current company will be lost. | The player can keep playing or save before replacing their session. | AIR-463 |
| LEAD-012 | 2026-11-21 | When an airline is already open, submitting a valid New Game form requires explicit confirmation that names the current and proposed airlines and explains that unsaved progress will be lost. | The player gets a clear chance to keep playing or go back and save before replacing the current airline. | AIR-465 |
| LEAD-013 | 2026-11-21 | When an airline is open, closing the desktop window requires confirmation that names the airline and warns that unsaved progress will be lost. The clock holds while the prompt is open, so the player can cancel and use the existing Save flow from the same moment. | The player can recover from an accidental window close and save before quitting. | AIR-467 |
| LEAD-014 | 2026-11-21 | When an active schedule loses qualified crew, its Attention entry opens Staff at the shortage and hiring action, and Staff identifies the affected aircraft and what crew is missing. | The player can restore service using the existing hiring rules without searching through Staff or rebuilding the schedule. | AIR-469 |
| LEAD-015 | 2026-11-21 | Leaving the New Game form with Cancel returns the player to the same company screen and working context, with keyboard focus on the action that opened the form. | A player who decides to keep their airline can pick up the work they were doing before opening New Game. | AIR-473 |
| LEAD-016 | 2026-11-21 | When a time jump stops for an Attention item that is still active, the clock bar offers a keyboard-accessible action to open that exact item's existing destination, even with Attention collapsed or several warnings present. The action disappears once the item clears, and a later jump, load or New Game removes it. | The player can act on the interruption directly instead of finding the matching row in the Attention list. | AIR-475 |
| LEAD-017 | 2026-11-21 | Entering financial distress and entering restructuring are distinct actionable Attention states. A time jump that reaches either stage stops and lets the player open the existing financing controls directly to understand the restriction or act before the restructuring deadline. | The player learns about a restriction or deadline at the moment it begins and can reach the rescue and financing actions without searching. | AIR-477 |
| LEAD-018 | 2026-11-21 | Load shows the saved airline name and home airport on each identifiable manual save, autosave and backup. A replacement confirmation identifies the current airline and that exact target generation so a player with several companies can tell which one they are about to open. | A player with several companies, reused save names or an autosave and backup from different companies can tell which airline a save will open before discarding progress. | AIR-479 |
| LEAD-019 | 2026-11-21 | In Routes, when an origin and aircraft are selected, destinations within that aircraft's reference range appear first, nearest first, while the rest stay available for comparison and are labeled out of range. If no catalogue destination is in range, Routes says so before the player picks one and explains that a longer-range aircraft can help. | The player can recover from an aircraft choice without testing airports one by one. | AIR-481 |
| LEAD-020 | 2026-11-21 | When Routes is blocked because the player has no aircraft or the selected aircraft cannot reach any catalogue destination from the chosen origin, Routes offers a direct action to open Fleet Procurement and keeps the route draft for the player's return. | The player can get an aircraft and resume planning from the same airports. | AIR-483 |
| LEAD-021 | 2026-11-21 | In the new-route planner, if the selected unassigned aircraft is parked away from the proposed origin, Routes shows its current airport and lets the player open the existing ferry confirmation with the aircraft and proposed origin preselected. Creation waits until the selected aircraft is ready at the origin so the player can reposition it before a route assignment prevents the ferry. | The player can reposition an aircraft and create the route from the same draft instead of discovering the location mismatch when activating the schedule. | AIR-485 |
| LEAD-022 | 2026-11-21 | To add an unassigned aircraft to an existing route, the player must first have it parked at the route's origin. When the aircraft is elsewhere, the route editor offers the existing ferry confirmation for that aircraft and origin and keeps both selections, so the player can finish the assignment after landing and ground time without losing their place. | The player can expand or restore a service with a spare aircraft elsewhere without a stale preview or a rejected or blocked assignment at scheduling time. | AIR-487 |
| LEAD-023 | 2026-11-21 | When a ferry requested from Routes is blocked by a shortage of available pilots with the required type rating, the confirmation shows the shortage and opens Staff with the Pilot role, required rating and missing count selected for explicit hiring. The route and aircraft selections stay available when the player returns so they can retry the ferry and finish the assignment they started. | The player can fix a missing pilot rating while planning a repositioning flight without losing their place or having crew hired on their behalf. | AIR-489 |
| LEAD-024 | 2026-11-21 | In Fleet, a pilot shortage still lets the player open the ferry confirmation, choose a destination and open Staff to review the required hire. Fleet keeps the aircraft and ferry destination for the retry so the player can reposition a newly acquired spare without rebuilding the request or hiring crew by guesswork. | The player can reposition a new spare aircraft from Fleet and hire the required crew explicitly instead of being blocked at the button. | AIR-491 |
| LEAD-025 | 2026-11-21 | Ferry confirmation checks the selected leg's destination and reference range before offering pilot hiring. It shows the actual destination blocker first, and the Staff shortcut appears only for a feasible leg whose current blocker is a pilot shortage. | A player does not commit to hiring pilots for a ferry they cannot fly. | AIR-493 |
| LEAD-026 | 2026-11-21 | The Finances company charts count charter fees as revenue and charter penalties as costs in the periods when they are booked, and show the result as the difference. | A player can reconcile changes over time with the cumulative company figures after accepting charter work. | AIR-495 |
| LEAD-027 | 2026-11-21 | When an active schedule cannot dispatch its next leg because assigned aircraft lack qualified crew, the Routes operations board shows that departure as blocked with no departure time until staffing is restored, while keeping the expected landing for any leg already in flight. | The player can distinguish a departure that needs action from a delay they can wait out. | AIR-497 |
